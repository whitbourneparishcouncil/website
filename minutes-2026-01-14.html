Parish Council Meeting Minutes: 14 January 2026

Download the minutes for 14 January 2026 (PDF, 2 pages, 497 KB)

Attendance:

Present: N Shaw (NS) (Chair); R. James (RJ); M. Clark (MC); P. Davis (PD); S. Knowles (SK);

P. Wood (PDW); L. Kershaw (LK)

In attendance: K. Phillips (Clerk). No members of the public present.

1. Apologies for absence.

P. Stoddart (PS) Ward Councillor and T. Hunt (TH) were not in attendance. J. Ashcroft (JA, formerly J Bromley (JB)) attended via Zoom and did not vote.

2. To receive declarations of interest.

There were no declarations of interest.

3. To approve the minutes of the Parish Council meeting held on 3rd December 2025.

Proposed PD, seconded RJ. Approved. All in favour.

4. Matters arising from the above minutes.

There were no matters arising.

5. Public Participation.

There were no members of the public present.

6. To receive report from Ward Councillor.

The Ward Councillor was not present to present his report for health reasons.

7. To receive the Clerk’s report.

  • The village hall has now been booked for the coming year.

  • The blocked drain down by the lodge has been reported and confirmed as a defect.

  • The precept has been submitted as agreed.

  • A parishioner was assisted with sheep and a fence.

  • The Webmaster is not responding to emails. The website domain name registration expired on the 11th January 2026. The supplier has been contacted to transfer the domain name to Whitbourne Parish Council should the need arise. The website is currently not available.

8. Financial matters.

  1. 8.1. To approve accounts for payment.

    Clerk Salary & HMRC PAYE, Clerk Expenses and Lengthsman. Details of expenditure are contained in a table at the end of these minutes. Proposed SK, seconded MC. All in favour. Approved.

  2. 8.2. To approve bank reconciliation to 31st December 2025 and

  3. 8.3. To approve Income and Expenditure reconciliation to 31st December 2025. Proposed LK, seconded PDW. All in favour. Approved.

  4. 8.4. To review current 2024/2025 budget versus spend. No comments raised.

9. Planning Consultations

  1. 9.1. 251015 - Summers Place, Badley Wood, Whitbourne. It was noted that this was a retrospective application. Policy RA6 of the Herefordshire Local Plan and Policies LU2 and LU9 of the Whitbourne Neighbourhood Development Plan were used as a basis for the discussion. It was proposed (PDW) and seconded (MC) to object to this application. However, the menage and holiday let aspects are supported subject to some requested planning conditions. All in favour. Decided.

10. To receive report regarding the Lengthsman Scheme

PDW reported that the Lengthsman is quite busy at the moment and is fitting things in. The verge on Stocking Lane has now been filled. Following grant approval from Herefordshire Council, the drainage work at the bottom of church bank will begin in March. PDW has once again approached the salt bin supplier with little progress. However, he has approached Herefordshire Council and has received a positive response about replacing two of the salt bins.

11. To determine approach to NDP review and group setup

There had been a small number of enquiries about joining the NDP working group. Previous members will be approached to enquire if they would like to join the new group.

12. To receive reports from the Village Hall, Playing Fields, and Manorial Court

  • Village Hall. Bookings are healthy and the finances are looking sound. The Christmas event went well. The committee is keen to purchase a projector as this may help bookings for business meetings and so on. There is a question over the stage being removed and costs are being sought.

  • Playing Fields. There have been no meetings since last time. A bangers and mash quiz night in March is being considered. The bingo night was not well attended.

  • Manorial Court. At the BCMC ordinary meeting, the main discussion focused on the management of Bringsty Common and the development of a management plan with the aim of securing future funding and maintaining the Common’s biodiversity and accessibility.

13. To receive reports regarding the Glebe/woodlands

PD reported that matters had gone quiet of late. There are two issues of concern, namely the trees that have been highlighted for felling and a telegraph pole that is leaning towards a property.

14. To report village issues regarding road maintenance, clearance, etc.

  • Flooding on the A44 by the junction with the C1066 has been very bad of late.

  • The oak tree on Stocking Lane that has been marked to be trimmed is close to an overhead wire and with high winds, the overhanging limb may pull down the cable.

  • The telegraph pole down over the grit bin on Stocking Lane has still not been removed.

  • Potholes in the dip by the lodge.

The Clerk requested agreement for a Clerk Delegation to pay the late-incoming Village Hall invoice. This was agreed and will be noted in the minutes of the next meeting.

The meeting closed at 21:02.
