Accessibility statement for Whitbourne Parish Council

This statement applies to the Whitbourne Parish Council website at whitbourneparishcouncil.org.uk and the documents we publish there.

Whitbourne Parish Council is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 Level AA due to the non-compliances listed below.

Non-accessible content

Non-compliance with the accessibility regulations

  • Finance / AGAR packs. The 2023/24 and 2024/25 PDFs do not have structural tags for screen readers. Tables in the 2025/26 transparency pack do not identify their headings to screen readers. The 2024/25 accounts have no document title or declared language. The 2025/26 pack also has no declared language. This affects WCAG 2.2 success criteria 1.3.1 (Info and Relationships), 2.4.2 (Page Titled) and 3.1.1 (Language of Page). The Finance / AGAR page links to HTML tables for accounting boxes 1 to 10 for all three years. The full packs and supporting material are available as PDFs.
  • Whitbourne Neighbourhood Development Plan. The Plan PDF does not identify its structure or language to assistive software. Its cover includes text as an image, and the policy maps do not have accessible text equivalents. This affects WCAG 2.2 success criteria 1.1.1 (Non-text Content), 1.3.1 (Info and Relationships) and 3.1.1 (Language of Page). Please use the contact arrangements below if you need information from the Plan in an accessible format.
  • Some other PDFs. The interests table in the Councillors’ Code of Conduct PDF does not identify the table headings to screen readers. This affects WCAG 2.2 success criterion 1.3.1 (Info and Relationships). An accessible HTML reading version of the Councillors’ Code of Conduct is available. You can also use the HTML reading links on the Regulations & Policies page, in the Diary and in the Agendas & Minutes archive.

Feedback and contact information

If you experience difficulty accessing information on this website or require information in another format, please email the Clerk at clerk@whitbourneparishcouncil.org.uk.

We aim to acknowledge enquiries within five working days and will respond as soon as reasonably practicable. If a request requires further investigation or work, we will explain what action is proposed and provide an indication of the likely timescale.

Complaints and enforcement

If you are unhappy with our response to an accessibility report or request, contact the Clerk. You can read the Council’s Complaints Procedure online.

The Equality and Human Rights Commission enforces the accessibility regulations in England. If you are unhappy with how we respond, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service. The enforcement procedure is set out in Part 5 of the accessibility regulations.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on 21 November 2026.

The website, including the accessibility improvements prepared in staging, was assessed on 21 November 2026 against WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The assessment included automated checks of links and website files, browser testing of responsive layouts and keyboard navigation, review of PDF structure and HTML reading versions, and manual testing of Safari browser zoom and VoiceOver. The review covered the main website sections and their navigation, policies, meeting records and finance documents.
